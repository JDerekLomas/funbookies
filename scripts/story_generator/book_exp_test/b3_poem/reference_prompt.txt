9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dreamy, atmospheric, soft-edged watercolor style.

Row 1 - THE CHILD (Curious Narrator):
[1] Character front view: A small child in a bright yellow raincoat and blue rubber boots. Large, expressive eyes, curious and gentle expression. Simple, rounded shapes. Background: soft cream wash.
[2] Character expressions: Same child showing three faces - quiet wonder (looking down), sudden surprise (eyebrows raised), and playful joy (wide smile).
[3] Character in action: The child walking slowly and carefully across damp, textured sand, looking intently at the ground.

Row 2 - THE PUDDLE AND THE SPLASH:
[4] The Spot: A perfectly still, clear puddle resting on dark, damp sand. The surface is like a mirror, reflecting a bright, soft-edged yellow sun. 
[5] The Drop: A single, translucent blue raindrop captured at the moment it hits the puddle's surface, creating delicate, concentric ripples that break the sun's reflection.
[6] KEY IMAGE: A dynamic, close-up shot of a blue boot stomping directly into the center of the puddle. Water splashes upward in soft, translucent blue droplets and golden light fragments.

Row 3 - THE PLAYGROUND SETTING (simplified):
[7] Damp Sand: A textural watercolor wash of tan, sienna, and grey. Hints of small pebbles and moisture glistening under a spring sun.
[8] Recess Atmosphere: A soft-focus background of a playground with a distant slide and swings. Bright, hazy spring sky with pale blue and white washes.
[9] Final scene: The child standing triumphantly next to the now-muddy and rippling puddle, looking up at a bright, warm sun with a happy, messy face.

STYLE: Dreamy, atmospheric children's book illustration. Soft watercolor washes with gentle transitions and feathered edges. Similar to the style of Jerry Pinkney or the softest illustrations of Tasha Tudor. Color palette: Bright raincoat yellow, sky blue, sandy browns, and shimmering gold. Fluid, rounded shape language. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
