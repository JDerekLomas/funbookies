9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, tide pool setting elements, coastal color palette. Narrative style with naturalistic marine details.

Row 1 - SHELLY (Small curious hermit crab):
[1] Shelly front view: Tiny hermit crab with delicate legs, large expressive eyes, current shell slightly too small. Soft beige and pale orange tones. Slightly hunched posture. Sand-colored background.
[2] Shelly expressions: Same character showing nervous/worried look, determined/clever smile, relieved/triumphant grin
[3] Shelly in action: Carefully navigating between tide pool rocks, moving cautiously with shell partially extended

Row 2 - SECONDARY ELEMENTS AND KEY MOMENT:
[4] Big Hermit Crab: Large dark-shelled crab with prominent black claws, towering over smaller characters. Shadowy silhouette with deep marine blues
[5] Tide Pool Environment: Intricate rocky landscape with coral fragments, wet stones, small marine details like starfish and anemones
[6] KEY IMAGE: Multiple hermit crabs exchanging shells, sunlight glinting off shell surfaces, creating a collaborative moment

Row 3 - SETTING:
[7] Dawn Tide Pool: Soft morning light, misty blues and pale oranges, gentle wave shadows
[8] Rock Textures: Detailed but stylized rocky surfaces with wet, glistening textures in muted greens and grays
[9] Final Scene: Hermit crabs settled in new shells, morning sunlight making shells shine like jewels

STYLE: Dynamic Eric Carle-inspired illustration with simplified marine shapes. Textured paper collage effect. Soft marine color palette: sage greens, sandy beiges, ocean blues, sunrise oranges. Rounded but intentional shapes. Friendly and approachable marine ecosystem illustration.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
