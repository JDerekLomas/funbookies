9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dreamy and atmospheric poetic style.

Row 1 - THE GENTLE SAILOR (A kind fisherman representing "They"):
[1] Character front view: A stout, friendly sailor wearing a bright yellow rain hat and a chunky navy blue knit sweater. Simple round shapes, big expressive eyes, a warm smile. Light tan skin and a soft grey beard. [Background color: soft lavender]
[2] Character expressions: Same sailor showing three faces - focused while steering, a wide yawn (sleepy), and a gentle look of wonder.
[3] Character in action: The sailor tossing a thick rope from the ship onto a dark wooden dock.

Row 2 - THE LONG SHIP AND THE SHELL:
[4] The Long Ship: A sturdy wooden fishing boat with a tall mast and dark mahogany wood. It has a rounded hull and sits low in the water. 
[5] The White Shell: A large, luminous white spiral shell resting on a dark, pebbled path. It seems to glow with its own inner light.
[6] KEY IMAGE: The silhouette of the long ship pulling into the dark dock, a massive silver moon reflecting in the rippling water, creating a "shimmer."

Row 3 - THE VILLAGE AT NIGHT (simplified):
[7] The Quiet Shack: A small wooden shack with a slanted roof and a shut door. Dark charcoal wood with soft blue shadows. A single lantern hanging unlit.
[8] The Rich Water: Deep indigo and teal watercolor washes representing "thick" water, with the faint, shimmering silver silhouette of a fish beneath the surface.
[9] Final scene: The white shell on the path in the foreground, the dark village shacks behind it, all under a vast, starry night sky.

STYLE: Dreamy, atmospheric children's book illustration with soft watercolor washes and gentle transitions. Inspired by the soft edges of Beatrix Potter but with a modern, nocturnal color palette. Deep indigo, navy blue, charcoal, and teal contrasted with moonlight silver, pale yellow, and stark white. Soft, rounded shape language. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
