9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dynamic, expressive, clear action.

Row 1 - PIP (Small bird/creature):
[1] Pip front view: Small, vibrant oxpecker bird with a bright orange beak, tiny round body, and thin legs. Simple geometric shapes, big expressive eyes. Light blue background.
[2] Pip expressions: Same Pip showing three faces - wide-eyed curiosity, startled surprise with ruffled feathers, and a happy squinting smile.
[3] Pip in action: Pip standing alert on a single thin reed, leaning forward as if about to hop.

Row 2 - STOMP AND THE SNACK:
[4] Stomp: A massive, rounded grey hippopotamus with a textured, pebbly skin. Only his broad back and watchful eyes peek out from the water.
[5] The Snack: A small, iridescent dragonfly with shimmering translucent wings, shown in a "zip" motion with slight blur lines.
[6] KEY IMAGE: The contrast of scale; the tiny Pip standing on the massive, wet, grey head of Stomp as a zipping insect circles between them in the bright sun.

Row 3 - RIVERBANK SETTING (simplified):
[7] Reeds and Mud: Thick, textured stalks of olive green reeds growing out of dark, damp, steaming brown mud.
[8] Sun and Sand: A large, solid bright yellow sun hanging over crisp, textured ochre sand. Heat haze ripples in the air.
[9] Final scene: Stomp and Pip together in the slow green water, surrounded by reeds, looking peaceful under the midday heat.

STYLE: Simple stylized children's book illustration with textured collage elements like Eric Carle and Leo Lionni. Color palette: Ochre, bright yellow, muddy green, slate grey, and vibrant turquoise. Bold graphic shapes and layered textures. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
