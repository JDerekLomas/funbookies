About FunBookies

Science of Reading aligned decodable books for emerging readers

What We Do

FunBookies creates decodable books that follow a systematic phonics progression. Unlike traditional leveled readers, our books are designed so that children can decode every word using the phonics patterns they've already learned.

Each book is carefully crafted to introduce one new phonics element at a time, building confidence and reinforcing the neural pathways that lead to fluent reading.

Our Approach

  • 1
    One Change Rule — Each level introduces only one new phonics element
  • 2
    Decodability First — 85-100% of words can be sounded out with taught patterns
  • 3
    Orthographic Mapping — Target words appear 4+ times for automatic recognition
  • 4
    Sight Word Discipline — High-frequency words introduced gradually with explicit caps
  • 5
    Engaging Stories — Real narratives with characters, problems, and resolutions
