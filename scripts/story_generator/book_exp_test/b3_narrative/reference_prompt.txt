9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dynamic, expressive narrative style.

Row 1 - PIP (Small oxpecker bird):
[1] Pip front view: Small, agile bird with brown feathers, a bright yellow and red beak, and large, curious expressive eyes. Simple geometric shapes. [Solid cream background]
[2] Pip expressions: Same bird showing three faces - wide-eyed curiosity, startled surprise, and a helpful smile.
[3] Pip in action: Pip mid-flight, wings spread, diving down toward the mud.

Row 2 - HUGO AND THE ITCHY SPOTS:
[4] Hugo the Hippo: A massive, grey, rounded hippo resembling a smooth river stone or stump. Small ears, sleepy eyes, thick textured skin.
[5] The Red Spots: Close-up of Hugo’s grey skin with tiny, bright red crawling insects grouped together.
[6] KEY IMAGE: Pip the bird landing on Hugo’s massive grey back which is poking out of thick green mud, under a large, vibrant yellow sun.

Row 3 - THE RIVERBANK (simplified):
[7] The Hot Sand: Textured, bright orange and yellow sand with a heavy, "stamped" sun icon in the corner.
[8] The Green Mud: Thick, olive-green water and mud with tall, vertical reed shapes rustling in the background.
[9] Resolution: Pip perched on Hugo's head, both looking peaceful. Hugo is submerged in the green water with only his eyes and Pip visible.

STYLE: Bold, textured collage style with hand-painted paper textures similar to Eric Carle and Leo Lionni. Saturated earth tones: ochre, olive green, slate grey, and pops of crimson red. Distinct, layered shapes with visible edges. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
