9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Narrative style with dynamic action and clear textures.

Row 1 - PATCH (A large, vulnerable hermit crab):
[1] Patch front view: A large, bright orange crab squeezed into a tiny, cracked white shell. Big expressive eyes, thin and soft legs poking out, looking uncomfortable. Simple geometric shapes, hand-painted paper texture. [Soft blue background]
[2] Patch expressions: Same crab showing three faces - Pain/Pinch (eyes squinted), Terror (looking upward at the sky), and Determination (claws forward).
[3] Patch in action: Patch scurrying quickly across wet sand, leaving his tiny shell behind, body looking soft and translucent.

Row 2 - THE THREAT AND THE MOMENT:
[4] The Gull: A stylized seagull shown as a sharp, black silhouette with wide wings, a yellow beak, and a singular piercing eye. 
[5] The Shells: A comparison of the "small shack" (a tiny, cramped spiral shell) and the "long rock" (a large, safe granite crevice with seaweed).
[6] KEY IMAGE: Patch dashing across a narrow sandy path between tide pools, a massive black wing shadow looming over him, the thin yellow sun rising on the horizon.

Row 3 - THE TIDE POOL (simplified):
[7] Tide pool details: Close-up of pink anemones, purple starfish, and green sea lettuce under clear, shallow water. Collage style with rich textures.
[8] Dawn atmosphere: The coastal horizon with a thin, pale yellow sun, cool misty blue air, and long purple shadows on the rocks.
[9] Resolution: Patch tucked safely and deeply into a dark, protective rock crevice, looking out with a sense of relief and calm.

STYLE: Simple stylized children's book illustration like Eric Carle and Leo Lionni. Hand-painted paper collage textures, bold graphic shapes, visible brushstrokes on paper cutouts. Color palette of dawn gold, deep ocean blues, sandy beige, and vibrant crab orange. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
