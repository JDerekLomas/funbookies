📚 Guided Letter Lesson

You'll teach letter sounds using the Direct Instruction method — the most effective way to teach reading.

1

Model

"I do it" — You say the sound clearly while your child watches and listens.

2

Lead

"We do it" — Say the sound together with your child.

3

Test

"You do it" — Your child says the sound alone. Praise or correct.
