## US Common Core Standards

### Overview

The Common Core State Standards for Reading: Foundational Skills (RF) cover:
- **Print Concepts** - Understanding how print works
- **Phonological Awareness** - Sound awareness without letters
- **Phonics and Word Recognition** - Letter-sound relationships
- **Fluency** - Accurate, automatic reading

Sources:
- [Common Core Reading Foundational Skills](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RF/)
- [Kindergarten RF Standards](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RF/K/)
- [Grade 1 RF Standards](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RF/1/)
- [Grade 2 RF Standards](https://www.thecorestandards.org/ELA-Literacy/RF/2/)

## Texas TEKS Standards

### Overview

The Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) for English Language Arts and Reading (ELAR) emphasize systematic phonics instruction from Kindergarten through Grade 3.

Source: [Texas Education Agency TEKS](https://tea.texas.gov/academics/curriculum-standards/teks)

### Kindergarten ELAR Phonics (§110.2)

#### Foundational Language Skills

| TEKS | Description | FunBookies Activity |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| K.2A | Identify and match the common sounds that letters represent | letter-drill, say-the-sound |
| K.2B | Use letter-sound relationships to decode, including CVC words | word-builder, blend-it |
| K.2C | Demonstrate phonological awareness (syllables, phonemes) | chop-it-up, sound-boxes |
| K.2D | Identify and read high-frequency words | sight-words |
| K.2E | Develop vocabulary through listening | (content exposure) |

#### Beginning Reading Skills/Phonics

| TEKS | Description | FunBookies Activity |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| K.2B(i) | Identify syllables in spoken words | chop-it-up |
| K.2B(ii) | Blend syllables to form multisyllabic words | blend-it |
| K.2B(iii) | Blend spoken phonemes to form one-syllable words | blend-it, voice-blend |
| K.2B(iv) | Manipulate phonemes within a base word | word-chains |

## UK Letters and Sounds Phases

### Overview

Letters and Sounds is a systematic synthetic phonics programme used across the UK, organized into 6 phases from pre-school through Year 2.

Sources:
- [Phonics Phases - PhonicsBloom](https://www.phonicsbloom.com/uk/article/phonics-phases)
- [Letters and Sounds DfE Publication](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7aa7b6e5274a34770e630c/Letters_and_Sounds_-_DFES-00281-2007.pdf)

### Phase 1: Aspect Development (Pre-Reading)

**Age**: Nursery/Reception (3-5 years)
**Focus**: Phonological awareness without letters

| Aspect | Skills | FunBookies Activity |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| Environmental sounds | Listening and remembering | (audio activities) |
| Instrumental sounds | Distinguishing sounds | (audio activities) |
| Body percussion | Creating sound patterns | (audio activities) |
| Rhythm and rhyme | Rhyme awareness | rhyme-match |
| Alliteration | Initial sound awareness | say-the-sound |
| Voice sounds | Oral blending/segmenting | blend-it, chop-it-up |

### Phase 2: First GPCs

**Age**: Reception (4-5 years)
**Duration**: ~6 weeks
**Focus**: Introduction of first 19 graphemes

| Set | Letters | Words Introduced |
|-----|---------|------------------|
| 1 | s, a, t, p | at, sat, pat, tap, sap |
| 2 | i, n, m, d | in, it, is, nip, tin |
| 3 | g, o, c, k | got, on, not, pot, cop |
| 4 | ck, e, u, r | duck, egg, up, run |
| 5 | h, b, f, ff, l, ll, ss | hat, but, puff, doll |

**Tricky Words**: the, to, I, no, go

**FunBookies Activities**: letter-drill, say-the-sound, blend-it, word-builder

### Phase 3: More GPCs

**Age**: Reception (4-5 years)
**Duration**: ~12 weeks
**Focus**: Remaining letters + digraphs

| Type | Graphemes |
|------|-----------|
| Letters | j, v, w, x, y, z, zz, qu |
| Consonant digraphs | ch, sh, th, ng |
| Vowel digraphs | ai, ee, igh, oa, oo (long), oo (short), ar, or, ur, ow, oi, ear, air, ure, er |

**Tricky Words**: he, she, we, me, be, was, you, they, all, are, my, her

**FunBookies Activities**: letter-drill, say-the-sound, word-builder, word-families

### Phase 4: Consonant Clusters

**Age**: Reception/Year 1 (5-6 years)
**Duration**: ~4-6 weeks
**Focus**: No new GPCs; practice blending and segmenting with adjacent consonants

| Cluster Position | Examples |
|------------------|----------|
| Initial blends | CVCC: went, help, jump |
| Final blends | CCVC: trip, spot, plan |
| Both | CCVCC: trunk, blend |

**Tricky Words**: said, have, like, so, do, some, come, were, there, little, one, when, out, what

**FunBookies Activities**: word-builder, blend-it, sound-boxes, word-chains

### Phase 5: Alternative Spellings

**Age**: Year 1 (5-6 years)
**Duration**: ~30 weeks
**Focus**: Alternative graphemes for known phonemes; alternative pronunciations

| Phoneme | Alternative Spellings |
|---------|-----------------------|
| /ai/ | ay, a-e, eigh, ey, ei |
| /ee/ | ea, e-e, ie, y, ey |
| /igh/ | ie, i-e, y, i |
| /oa/ | ow, o-e, oe, o |
| /oo/ | ew, ue, u-e, ou |
| /or/ | aw, au, ore, oar |
| /er/ | ir, ur, ear |
| /ow/ | ou |
| /oi/ | oy |

**Alternative Pronunciations**:
- a: cat, acorn, was
- e: bed, he
- i: it, find
- o: hot, cold, today
- u: but, put
- ow: cow, blow
- ie: tie, field
- ea: eat, bread, break
- er: her, farmer
- ch: chip, chef, school
- y: yes, by, very
- ou: out, you, could, shoulder

**FunBookies Activities**: word-builder, say-the-sound, sight-words, read-aloud

### Phase 6: Fluency and Accuracy

**Age**: Year 2 (6-7 years)
**Duration**: Throughout Year 2
**Focus**: Fluent application, spelling patterns, longer words

| Skill | Focus Areas |
|-------|-------------|
| Suffixes | -s, -es, -ed, -er, -est, -ing, -ful, -ly, -ment, -ness |
| Prefixes | un-, dis-, re-, pre- |
| Spelling rules | Doubling, dropping -e, changing y to i |
| Syllable types | Open, closed, VCe, vowel team, r-controlled, consonant-le |

**FunBookies Activities**: word-builder, read-aloud, sentence-scramble

## Scope and Sequence

### Recommended Lesson Progression

Based on alignment across all three standards systems:

#### Foundation (Lessons 1-6) - Phase 2 / Kindergarten
1. s, a, t, p
2. i, n, m, d
3. g, o, c, k
4. ck, e, u, r
5. h, b, f, l
6. ff, ll, ss, j, v, w, x, y, z, qu

#### Digraphs (Lessons 7-12) - Phase 3 / Late K - Early Grade 1
7. ch, sh
8. th, ng
9. ai, ee
10. igh, oa
11. oo (long/short), ar
12. or, ur, er

#### Blends (Lessons 13-18) - Phase 4 / Grade 1
13. Initial blends: st, sp, sm, sn
14. Initial blends: bl, cl, fl, gl, pl, sl
15. Initial blends: br, cr, dr, fr, gr, pr, tr
16. Final blends: nd, nk, nt, mp
17. Final blends: ft, lt, lk, pt, sk
18. CCVCC words

#### Advanced Patterns (Lessons 19-30) - Phase 5 / Grade 1-2
19. ay, a-e (alternative /ai/)
20. ea, e-e, ie (alternative /ee/)
21. ie, i-e, y (alternative /igh/)
22. ow, o-e, oe (alternative /oa/)
23. ew, ue, u-e (alternative /oo/)
24. aw, au, ore, oar (alternative /or/)
25. ir, ur, ear (alternative /er/)
26. oy, oi (diphthongs)
27. ou, ow (diphthongs)
28. Soft c (ce, ci, cy)
29. Soft g (ge, gi, gy)
30. Silent letters (kn, wr, gn, mb)

#### Fluency (Lessons 31+) - Phase 6 / Grade 2
31-36. Suffixes and inflections
37-42. Prefixes
43+. Multi-syllable words and fluency
