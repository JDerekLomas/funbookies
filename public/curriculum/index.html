Implementation Guides

Phonics Foundation (0-8)

Pre-readers through r-controlled vowels. Focus on systematic decoding with 95%+ decodability.

  • CVC patterns (levels 1-2)
  • Digraphs and blends (levels 3-5)
  • Silent e and vowel teams (levels 6-7)
  • R-controlled vowels (level 8)

Transitional (9-12)

Diphthongs through 2-syllable words. Bridge from decoding to fluency with 90%+ decodability.

  • Complex vowel patterns (levels 9-11)
  • Syllable division rules (level 12)
  • Compound words introduced

Developing (13-16)

Morphology focus: suffixes, prefixes, Latin roots. Vocabulary development becomes central.

  • All 6 syllable types (level 13)
  • Common affixes (levels 14-15)
  • Latin root words (level 16)

Fluent & Advanced (17-23)

Academic vocabulary, complex syntax, figurative language, and literary analysis.

  • Greek roots and Tier 2 vocab (levels 17-18)
  • Complex sentences (level 19)
  • Figurative language (level 20)
  • Domain/literary analysis (levels 21-23)
