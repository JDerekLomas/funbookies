About Our Research

FunBookies is developing phonics-based learning activities for early readers. Our experiments help us make evidence-based decisions about content quality, voice selection, and pedagogical approaches.

All participation is voluntary and anonymous. We collect only your preferences and optional demographic information to understand different perspectives.
