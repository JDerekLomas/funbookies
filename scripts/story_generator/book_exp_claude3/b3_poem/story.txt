# Splash and Stomp
Level: B3 | Mode: poem

**Plip-Plop Symphony**

*Plip.*
*Plop.*
*Splash.*

Crisp spring air snaps.
Spots on the blacktop—
big, wet spots that grin up at me.

Step, step, step to the best spot.
Stop.
Stomp!

Water grins up, up, UP—
drops like glass bits
snap back down.
*Plip-plop-plip.*

"I can make it jump!"
Stomp, stomp.
The spot grabs my boot,
scrubs the mud off,
spits wet drops that spin and—
*splat.*

Snap of wet steps on blacktop.
Crisp drops stick to my hands,
cold as morning breath.

Just one plan.
Trust the plop.
Trust the way water
splits apart
but snaps back
to one
glad
spot.

*Plip.*
*Plop.*
*Still.*
