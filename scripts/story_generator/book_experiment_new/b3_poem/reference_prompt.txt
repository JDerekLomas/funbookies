9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dreamy, atmospheric visual storytelling for a poem.

Row 1 - THE ALLIGATOR (A friendly, green protagonist):
[1] Alligator front view: Friendly bright green alligator with a pale yellow belly, big expressive eyes, short sturdy legs, and a long tail. Simple rounded shapes, soft textures. Off-white background.
[2] Alligator expressions: Same alligator showing three faces - Focused (brows slightly knit), Surprised (eyes wide, mouth a small 'o'), and Wide Grin (eyes closed happily, showing a few blunt teeth).
[3] Alligator in action: The alligator plodding slowly through damp, heavy sand, leaving deep, textured footprints behind.

Row 2 - WATER AND ACTION:
[4] The Prey: A small, vibrant blue dragonfly with translucent wings, hovering near the water's surface.
[5] The Puddle: A clear blue puddle reflecting the sky, featuring concentric ripple circles and "spinning" spots of water.
[6] KEY IMAGE: The Alligator mid-stomp in a large puddle, water splashing upward in soft blue watercolor arcs, mouth open in a playful "snap" and "chomp."

Row 3 - THE PLAYGROUND SETTING (simplified):
[7] Playground Sand: Textured damp tan sand with scattered small pebbles and tiny tufts of bright green spring grass at the edges.
[8] Spring Atmosphere: A soft pale blue sky with wispy white clouds and a gentle, hazy yellow sun, creating a fresh, post-rain mood.
[9] Final scene: The alligator resting peacefully, belly-down on a smooth, sun-warmed grey rock next to the puddle, looking satisfied and glad.

STYLE: Dreamy and atmospheric with soft watercolor washes and gentle transitions. Simple stylized children's book illustration with soft edges. Palette of spring greens, watery blues, sandy tans, and soft yellows. Rounded shape language. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
