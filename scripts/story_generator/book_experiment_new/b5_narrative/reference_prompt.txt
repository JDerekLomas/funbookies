9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dynamic, expressive collage style.

Row 1 - PIP (Small hermit crab):
[1] Pip front view: A small, bright orange hermit crab with two large, expressive black eyes on stalks. He has a soft, curled body partially tucked into a small, dark, jagged grey shell. Simple geometric shapes, friendly appearance. [Solid light teal background]
[2] Pip expressions: Same hermit crab showing three faces - one wincing with a "pinched" look, one wide-eyed with wonder, and one determined with a small smile.
[3] Pip in action: Pip trying to tuck his legs into his tiny shell, looking cramped and uncomfortable, shell tilted awkwardly.

Row 2 - THE DREAM SHELL AND DISCOVERY:
[4] The New Shell: A large, magnificent white spiral shell with a pearly luster, smooth curves, and a wide opening. 
[5] Tide Pool Friends: A textured purple sea star and a soft pink sea anemone with waving, rounded tentacles.
[6] KEY IMAGE: Pip standing atop a dark, wet rock silhouette against a thin pink sun rising over the ocean horizon, looking at a bright white flash in the shallow water.

Row 3 - COASTAL TIDE POOLS (simplified):
[7] Tide pool floor: Shallow clear water over a bed of colorful smooth pebbles, green sea lettuce, and small oxygen bubbles.
[8] Dawn atmosphere: The meeting of dark wet rocks and a soft pink and orange sky, misty and calm with gentle ripples.
[9] The Search: Pip scuttling across wet sand toward a glowing white object in the shallow surf, leaving tiny tracks behind him.

STYLE: Simple stylized children's book illustration with paper collage textures like Leo Lionni and Eric Carle. Vibrant orange, deep sea blue, dawn pink, and pearlescent white. Bold organic shapes and hand-painted paper textures. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
