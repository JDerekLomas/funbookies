### 2.5 Inflectional Endings (Band C6)

#### Lesson 32: -s, -es Plurals and Verbs
```
Band: C6
Patterns: Adding -s or -es
Rules:
  - Add -s to most words: cats, dogs, runs, jumps
  - Add -es after s, x, z, ch, sh: boxes, wishes, catches, buzzes
  - Change y to i and add -es: baby→babies, story→stories (but: boy→boys)
  - Change f to v and add -es: leaf→leaves, knife→knives (but: roof→roofs)
Words: cats, dogs, boxes, wishes, catches, babies, stories, leaves, wolves, churches, branches, brushes, buzzes,ixes
Activities: word-builder, word-sort, sentence-scramble
```

#### Lesson 33: -ed Past Tense
```
Band: C6
Patterns: Three sounds of -ed
Rules:
  - /t/ after voiceless consonants (p, k, f, s, sh, ch): jumped, walked, stuffed, missed, washed, watched
  - /d/ after voiced consonants and vowels: rubbed, hugged, called, named, played, tried
  - /ɪd/ after t or d: wanted, needed, landed, started, added, ended
Spelling rules:
  - Double final consonant: stop→stopped, plan→planned, hop→hopped
  - Drop silent e: hope→hoped, bake→baked, smile→smiled
  - Change y to i: try→tried, cry→cried, carry→carried
Activities: word-sort (by sound), word-builder, read-aloud
```

#### Lesson 34: -ing Present Participle
```
Band: C6
Patterns: Adding -ing
Rules:
  - Add -ing to most words: jump→jumping, play→playing, read→reading
  - Drop silent e: hope→hoping, make→making, write→writing
  - Double final consonant (CVC): run→running, sit→sitting, hop→hopping
  - Don't double w, x, y: snow→snowing, play→playing, box→boxing
Activities: word-builder, word-sort, sentence-scramble
```

#### Lesson 35: -er, -est Comparatives
```
Band: C6
```

## Activity Development

### New Activities Needed

#### 1. digraph-sort (Band B5)
```
Purpose: Sort words by digraph (sh, ch, th, wh, ng)
Gameplay:
  - Words appear one at a time
  - Player drags to correct digraph bucket
  - Audio plays digraph sound on correct sort
  - Track accuracy per digraph for confusion matrix
```

#### 2. syllable-sort (Bands C3-C4)
```
Purpose: Practice syllable division patterns
Gameplay:
  - Word appears with syllable break options
  - Player taps where to divide
  - Feedback shows correct division
  - Modes: VC/CV, V/CV, consonant-le
```

#### 3. morpheme-builder (Bands C6-C8)
```
Purpose: Build words from prefixes, roots, suffixes
Gameplay:
  - Target meaning shown (e.g., "not happy")
  - Morpheme tiles available (un-, happy, -ness)
  - Player builds: un + happy = unhappy
  - Extend: unhappy + -ness = unhappiness
```
