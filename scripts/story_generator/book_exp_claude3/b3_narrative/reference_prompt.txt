9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Narrative style with Eric Carle-inspired illustration.

Row 1 - Pip (Snap Bird):
[1] Pip front view: Small red-headed bird with crisp triangular beak, round body, bright curious eyes. Feathers in burnt orange and rust red tones. Delicate stick legs. Soft sand-colored background.
[2] Pip expressions: Same bird showing nervous flutter, determined snap, gentle contentment
[3] Pip in action: Bird perched on hippo's back, head tilted, beak reaching to pluck a tick

Row 2 - Secondary Elements and Key Moment:
[4] Hippo: Large rounded gray form, smooth skin with subtle texture, gentle eyes, broad back with muddy patches
[5] River setting: Slow green water, soft reed edges, hints of rippling surface
[6] KEY IMAGE: Pip as a bright red dot atop massive gray hippo back, mid-river with reeds framing scene

Row 3 - Setting:
[7] Riverbank: Hot sand in ochre and pale tan, scattered stones, heat shimmer
[8] Midday light: Intense golden sunlight, long shadows, crisp edge between light and dark
[9] Final scene: Hippo and bird together, peaceful river background, soft green and warm browns

STYLE: Simplified naturalistic children's book illustration. Flat color blocks with textured edges. Soft geometric shapes. Warm earthy palette of rust, gray, green, tan. Bold silhouettes with gentle curves. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
