9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Poetic, dreamy watercolor style.

Row 1 - PUDDLE STOMPER (Playful child protagonist):
[1] Child front view: Young elementary student in bright yellow rain boots, light blue raincoat, wild windblown hair. Slightly messy, joyful appearance. Soft gray-blue background.
[2] Child expressions: Same child showing curiosity (wide eyes), delight (big grin), concentration (slight frown/determined look)
[3] Child in action: Dramatic mid-stomp pose, boot raised, water droplets spinning around feet

Row 2 - PUDDLE DYNAMICS:
[4] Puddle character: Irregular water shape with animated "face", droplets with personality, reflective surface
[5] Playground blacktop: Wet surface with irregular puddle shapes, gleaming texture
[6] KEY IMAGE: Water exploding upward from boot stomp, droplets frozen in mid-air like glass fragments

Row 3 - SETTING:
[7] Playground background: Soft-edged blacktop, misty spring morning light
[8] Rain atmosphere: Gentle water drops, soft gray-green color wash
[9] Final scene: Child and puddle in harmonious interaction, water droplets dancing

STYLE: Watercolor with soft edges like Julie Morstad. Muted spring palette - grays, blues, hints of yellow. Organic shapes with playful movement. Dreamy, slightly abstracted. Fluid transitions between forms.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
