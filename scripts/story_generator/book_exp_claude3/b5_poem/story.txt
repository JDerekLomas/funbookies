# The Shimmer and the Shell
Level: B5 | Mode: poem

**When Night Fish Wish**

Shush, shush, shush—
the waves rush and hush
at the dock.

Ships rock.
Shells click and clack
in the wet sand.

What is that?
A flash in the black water—
then much more.

The boy's hands
reach down, down,
and the sea gives back
chips of light,
rich blue sparks
that dance and dash
away from his touch.

Thick with magic.
Thin white streaks.
Swimming stars.

Each splash makes magic.
Each ripple brings
a rush of silver fire
that blooms
and fades
and blooms.

The moon looks down
from her dark chair
as the water whispers
its bright secrets
to the rocking ships,
the sleeping shacks,
the long night.

Shush, shush, shush—
the sea keeps
its white treasures,
then shares them
with those
who reach
into the black
and wish.
