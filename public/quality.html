How We Evaluate Books

Each book is scored on three main factors, combining AI analysis with programmatic checks

📖

Story Quality

50% of overall score

Claude AI reads the full story text and evaluates it against six criteria designed for early reader books (ages 4-7).

Coherence 1-5

Does the story make logical sense? Are there plot holes or confusing jumps between scenes?

Engagement 1-5

Is it interesting? Would a child want to keep reading or ask to hear it again?

Clarity 1-5

Is the narrative easy to follow? Is it clear what's happening on each page?

Emotional Arc 1-5

Does the story have satisfying progression? A clear beginning, middle, and end?

Age Appropriate 1-5

Is the content, vocabulary, and complexity right for early readers?

Educational Value 1-5

Does it teach something meaningful or reinforce reading skills?

⚙

Technical Completeness

25% of overall score

Programmatic checks verify the book has all required metadata for consistent image generation and future improvements.

Story Bible +1 point

Background document defining the world, setting, and narrative context.

Character Definitions +1 point

Detailed descriptions of each character's appearance for consistent illustrations.

Scene Descriptions +1-2 points

Rich visual descriptions for each page (not placeholder text like "Illustration for...").

Reference Prompt +1 point

Style guide prompt for generating consistent 9-panel reference sheets.

🗣

Phonics Alignment

25% of overall score

Analyzes vocabulary to ensure it matches the book's reading level and supports phonics instruction.

Word Complexity analyzed

Average word length should match level (Band A: ~3.5 letters, Band B: ~4.5 letters).

CVC Words +1 point if >30%

Consonant-Vowel-Consonant words (cat, dog, sit) are ideal for early readers.

Sight Words tracked

High-frequency words (the, is, and) that children memorize by sight.

Level Match pass/flag

Overall vocabulary complexity appropriate for the book's stated reading level.

Final Verdict Calculation

Good Score 4.0 - 5.0
Needs Improvement Score 2.5 - 3.9
Poor Score 1.0 - 2.4
Overall Score = (Story × 0.50) + (Technical × 0.25) + (Phonics × 0.25)
