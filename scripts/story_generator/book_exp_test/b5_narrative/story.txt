# The Shell Switch
Level: B5 | Mode: narrative

The sun is thin. The tide pool is a cold hush. 

This shell is much too small. His legs pinch. His back is a crush. He is a big crab in a small shack. 

Patch must dash. He is thin and soft. A gull is a black wing in the sun. He must rush to the long rock. He will dash on the path.
