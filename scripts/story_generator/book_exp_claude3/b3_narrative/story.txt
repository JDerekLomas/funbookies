# Ox and the Big Hip
Level: B3 | Mode: narrative

**The Snap Bird's First Day**

Hot sand burns Pip's claws as she steps to the big mud spot. Her crisp red beak snaps at air—snap, snap, snap. She must find bugs to eat, but the water looks so deep.

A plop echoes. Then another. PLOP.

Something big and gray rises from the mud like a rock. Water drips from its broad back. Pip's wings flutter, but her belly cramps.

The hippo grins. "What do you want, snap bird?"

Pip's voice is small. "I must eat."

"Step on my back," he rumbles. "Scrub the bugs from my spots. I trust you will not chomp me."

Pip steps on. The hippo's skin feels warm. She finds a fat tick and pulls—pop! Sweet juice fills her beak.

The hippo's breathing slows.

Pip scrubs and snaps, her red head bobbing like a bright flower in the crisp sunlight. The reeds whisper their approval.
