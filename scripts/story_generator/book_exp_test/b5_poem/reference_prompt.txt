9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dreamy, atmospheric, and soft-edged watercolor style.

Row 1 - THE NARRATOR (A curious young child):
[1] Narrator front view: A small child wearing a bright yellow raincoat and matching rain hat, dark curly hair peeking out, big expressive eyes full of wonder. Simple shapes, soft watercolor textures. [Background: Soft navy blue]
[2] Narrator expressions: Same child showing three faces - curiosity (head tilted), surprise (rounded mouth), and peaceful wonder (soft smile, eyes bright).
[3] Narrator in action: The child walking through thick, swirling white mist, one hand touching their chin where the mist gathers.

Row 2 - COASTAL TREASURES AND KEY MOMENT:
[4] The Shell: A detailed, iridescent spiral seashell resting on a dark, wet, textured rock. Soft glows of pink and pearl.
[5] The Ship: A small wooden fishing boat tied to a weathered dock, tilted slightly as if rocking on a gentle wave.
[6] KEY IMAGE: The child standing on the dark, wet sand at the water's edge, looking down in awe at a glowing shimmer in the rushing tide under a large, luminous moon.

Row 3 - THE NIGHT VILLAGE (simplified):
[7] The Shack: A small, simple wooden shack with a shut door and dark windows, silhouetted against a deep indigo night sky.
[8] The Path: A dark, winding coastal path through tall beach grass, shrouded in thick, soft-edged white mist.
[9] Final scene: The child standing peacefully on the shore, moonbeams reflecting on the rippling water, tiny sparkles in the sand.

STYLE: Dreamy and atmospheric poem style. Soft watercolor washes with gentle transitions and blurred edges. Color palette of deep indigo, midnight blue, misty silver, and seafoam green, with a bright pop of raincoat yellow. Soft, rounded shape language. Friendly and approachable. NOT realistic.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
