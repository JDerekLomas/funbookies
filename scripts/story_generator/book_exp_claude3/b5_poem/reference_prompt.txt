9-PANEL STYLE REFERENCE SHEET for children's picture book

FOCUS: Character design, setting elements, color palette. Dreamy, poetic nighttime illustration.

Row 1 - THE BOY (Young discoverer of sea magic):
[1] Boy front view: Slim 7-8 year old, tousled hair, rolled-up pants, curious expression. Silhouetted against moonlit water. Soft midnight blue undertones.
[2] Boy expressions: Same boy showing wonder, excitement, quiet awe. Large, luminous eyes reflecting sea sparkles.
[3] Boy in action: Leaning over dock edge, hands reaching into water, fingers trailing bioluminescent trails.

Row 2 - MAGICAL ELEMENTS:
[4] The Moon: Stylized female face, soft silver-white, gazing down from corner with gentle watching expression.
[5] Bioluminescent sea: Swirling blue-silver-white abstract shapes suggesting magical movement.
[6] KEY IMAGE: Boy's hand touching water, erupting with dancing silver-blue magical sparks.

Row 3 - SETTING:
[7] Fishing Dock: Weathered wooden planks, soft gray-blue shadows, subtle moonlight.
[8] Night Water: Deep indigo with luminescent streaks, gentle rippling texture.
[9] Full Scene: Boy, moon, boats, water in peaceful nighttime harmony.

STYLE: Soft watercolor with ethereal edges like Jane Ray. Luminescent color palette: midnight blues, silver, soft whites. Organic, fluid shape language. Dreamy and mysterious. Gentle watercolor transitions between elements.

LAYOUT: 3x3 grid, thin white borders between panels.

CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS anywhere. Pure illustration only.
